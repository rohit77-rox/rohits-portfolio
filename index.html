About Me

Where hardware meets software

I'm a final-year IT undergraduate pursuing a B.Voc in Information Technology (IoT & Data Science) at Christ College (Autonomous), Irinjalakuda — expected to graduate in January 2026. My work lives at the intersection of embedded systems and full-stack web development.

From building SmartDine 360 — an IoT-based restaurant management system integrating ESP32 hardware with a React/Firebase stack — to developing a real-time driver drowsiness detector using computer vision, I gravitate toward projects that solve real problems with both hardware and software.

Beyond engineering, I've led IoT workshops for high school students, served as Media Coordinator for college fests, and hold an NCC "B" Certificate. I'm currently open to graduate roles, internships, or freelance collaborations.
